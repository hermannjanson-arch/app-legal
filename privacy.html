What stays on your phone

Your goals, affirmations and gratitude entries, the pictures you add to goals, your practice days and streak, your favorite and own quotes, your reminders and your settings are stored only in the app's storage on your device.

The app has no user accounts and no server of its own. We cannot see, read or restore any of this content. If your phone makes device backups (for example iCloud or Google backup), this content can be part of your own backup, according to your phone settings.

You can export your content as a file, import it again, or delete everything under Settings → Your data. Uninstalling the app also deletes it from the device.

Permissions

Notifications: used only for the reminders you set. They are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us.

Photos: when you add a picture to a goal, you choose it yourself in your phone's photo picker. The app receives only that picture and keeps a copy in its own storage.

Face ID, fingerprint or passcode: if you switch on the app lock, your phone checks your identity. The app only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. With Premium or the one-time "Remove ads" purchase, no ads are loaded.

To deliver and measure ads, Google processes data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Details: https://policies.google.com/privacy

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your choice in a consent dialog before any ad is loaded. Personalized ads are shown only with your consent; otherwise ads are not personalized. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Legal → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone, the app additionally asks for permission to track through Apple's dialog; you can change this in the iOS settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG). You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are sold and billed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment details.

To check which purchases are active, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA). RevenueCat receives a random app user ID, your purchase history for this app as reported by the store, and technical data such as device type, operating system version, app version, country and IP address. Details: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Legal basis: performance of the contract with you (Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR). The data is kept for as long as it is needed to provide your purchases and to meet legal retention duties.

No analytics

The app contains no analytics or tracking tools of its own, and we do not create user profiles.

Transfers outside the EU

Google and RevenueCat may process data in the USA. Such transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified, and otherwise on the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the one responsible for your place of residence.

Because your content never leaves your phone, we cannot look it up or delete it for you; you control it yourself in the app. For everything else, write to the email address above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We update this policy when the app changes. The current version is always available in the app under Settings → Legal.
